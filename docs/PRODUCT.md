# Product Specification: ANOWLA Clinical

## 1. Product Mission
A dedicated study and retention platform for nursing students, NCLEX-RN candidates, medical residents, and healthcare professionals. The system converts raw clinical syllabi, clinical guidelines, and pharmacology drug cards into high-yield active-recall decks using spaced repetition.

## 2. Core Modules
- **Interactive Medical PDF Workspace**:
  - Upload textbook chapters, clinical protocols, and drug handbooks.
  - Browser-native text extraction with live note editing and text cleanup.
  - One-click active-recall generation using clinical unslop prompts.
  - Inline card refinement (Front, Back, Distractors, Rationale).
- **Clinical Folder Architecture**:
  - Create and manage clinical specialties (e.g., Pharmacology, Med-Surg, Maternal & Newborn, NCLEX-RN Prep).
  - Assign decks to folders with real-time deck counters.
- **Spaced Repetition Algorithm (SM-2)**:
  - Intervals calculated based on recall quality (Again, Hard, Good, Easy).
  - Daily review quotas to prevent study burnout during hospital clinicals.
- **Strict Clinical Scope**:
  - Zero math, zero generic computer programming.
  - Focus: Drug mechanisms, adverse effects, triage prioritization (EAT rules), lab values, and diagnostic indicators.
